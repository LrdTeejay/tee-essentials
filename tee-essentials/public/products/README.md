# Product photos

The store already shows real photos loaded from Unsplash (free to use under the Unsplash License:
https://unsplash.com/license). They are listed by product in `src/data/products.js` (the PHOTOS list).

To use your own pictures later, drop files in this folder (e.g. `heavyweight-tee-front.jpg`) and
replace that product's entries in PHOTOS with `'products/heavyweight-tee-front.jpg'`.
